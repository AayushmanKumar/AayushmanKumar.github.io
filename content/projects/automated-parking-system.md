# Automated Car Parking System

Compact automated parking module with custom 3D-printed rotor components, Arduino motor speed control, and embedded systems integration.

## Design & Engineering

- **Digital Fabrication**: Modeled and 3D-printed custom rotor assemblies using FDM PLA, optimized for strength-to-weight ratio.
- **Embedded Control**: Programmed Arduino microcontrollers for precise DC motor PWM speed and directional control.
- **Safety Interlocks**: Integrated optical limit switches and ultrasonic proximity sensors.

## Practical Outcome

Delivered a functional, compact rotary parking prototype capable of handling multi-tier platform movements up to 10 kg payloads.
